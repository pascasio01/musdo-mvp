====================================================
PLAYER PHILOSOPHY

The MUSVORA Player is not an entertainment player.

The MUSVORA Player is an Asset Review Player.

Purpose:

* Listen
* Review
* Verify
* Evaluate
* License

Every playback session must reinforce asset governance.

====================================================
PLAYER TYPES

1. Preview Player

Purpose:

* Demo listening
* Marketplace preview
* Asset review

Features:

* Play
* Pause
* Seek
* Duration
* Waveform
* Volume

====================================================

2. Secure Demo Player

Purpose:

Protect creator assets.

Options:

* Stream only
* Disable download
* Watermark ready architecture
* Preview duration limits
* Full access permissions

====================================================

3. Governance Player

Purpose:

Display asset intelligence while listening.

Display:

* Readiness Score
* Ownership Confidence
* Song Passport Status
* Licensing Status

Example:

Readiness: 91

Ownership: 84

Passport: Active

Licensing: Ready

====================================================

4. Lyrics Review Panel

Purpose:

Review lyrics and composition.

Tabs:

* Audio
* Lyrics
* Metadata
* Ownership

Support:

* Original Lyrics
* Translated Lyrics
* AI Translation (Review Recommended)

====================================================

5. Version Comparison Player

Purpose:

Compare versions.

Support:

* Demo V1
* Demo V2
* Demo V3
* Master

Switch instantly between versions.
